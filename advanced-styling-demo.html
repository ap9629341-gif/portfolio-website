🎯 Step 5: Custom Scrollbar Styling

Custom Scrollbar

Scroll down to see the custom scrollbar in action!

This is a long content area to demonstrate the custom scrollbar styling.

The scrollbar has a gradient thumb with rounded corners and hover effects.

Notice how the scrollbar track is light gray and the thumb has a blue gradient.

When you hover over the thumb, it changes to a darker blue gradient.

This creates a more polished and branded experience for users.

Custom scrollbars work in WebKit browsers (Chrome, Safari, Edge) and Firefox.

The styling includes width, track background, thumb gradient, and hover states.

Keep scrolling to see more content and test the scrollbar behavior.

This demonstrates attention to detail in UI/UX design.

Every small detail contributes to a professional user experience.

Custom Scrollbar Properties:

  • ::-webkit-scrollbar - Entire scrollbar
  • ::-webkit-scrollbar-track - Track/background
  • ::-webkit-scrollbar-thumb - Draggable part
  • scrollbar-width - Firefox property
